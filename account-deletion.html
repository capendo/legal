Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated data.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Capendo.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently removes your account identifier and your learning data (texts read, saved words, quiz results, XP, streak, language/level preferences) from our backend.

By email

If you can’t use the in-app option, email dpw.vanderhoeven.tech@gmail.com from the relevant device/account context and we will delete your data within 30 days and confirm.

What is deleted

Your account identifier and all learning progress tied to it. Short-term technical logs age out automatically.

What we may retain

Limited records we are legally required to keep (e.g. tax/transaction records for purchases, held by Apple as the seller). Subscription billing is managed by Apple — cancel any active subscription via Apple subscription settings; deleting your Capendo account does not cancel an Apple subscription.
