Frequently asked

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings inside the app and tap “Restore Purchases”, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Capendo, or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the current period ends.

I lost my progress / changed device

If you used Sign in with Apple, sign in again on the new device and your progress syncs. Anonymous device accounts are tied to the original device; contact us and we’ll help where we can.
