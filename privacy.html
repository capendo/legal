Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

Capendo (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a language-learning app that lets you read authentic texts and tap any word or sentence for an AI translation in context. We designed Capendo to collect as little personal data as possible. This policy explains exactly what we process and why.

Data controller: Daniel van der Hoeven, the Netherlands. Contact: dpw.vanderhoeven.tech@gmail.com.

1. Account & identity

By default Capendo creates an anonymous device account: a random identifier generated on your device, with no name, email, or phone number. We do not know who you are.

You may optionally use Sign in with Apple. In that case we receive only the identifier Apple provides and, if you allow it, a (possibly Apple-relayed, anonymised) email address. We never receive your Apple password. You can choose Apple’s “Hide My Email” relay; we honour it.

2. What we process

DataPurposeKept
Anonymous account ID (and Apple identifier if you sign in) Sync your progress across sessions/devices Until you delete your account
Learning progress: texts read, words you saved, quiz results, XP, streak, chosen learning language and level Provide the core learning experience and your path Until you delete your account
Words/sentences you tap to translate Sent to our backend and to an AI provider to generate the translation/explanation you requested Cached briefly to speed up and reduce cost; not tied to a profile for any other purpose
Subscription status (active / trial / expired) Unlock premium features; enforce limits While your account exists
Basic technical logs (timestamps, error/diagnostic data) Security, abuse prevention, fixing bugs Short-term, rotated

We do not collect contacts, photos, location, advertising identifiers, or browsing history outside the app. We do not show ads and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

3. AI processing

When you tap a word or sentence, that text (with limited surrounding context from what you are reading) is sent to our backend and forwarded to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the translation and explanation. This text is processed to answer your request and is not used to train AI models. We send only the text needed for the translation, not your identity.

4. Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card or full payment details. We use RevenueCat as a processor to receive Apple’s subscription status so we can unlock premium features. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

5. Processors & where data is handled

  • Cloudflare: application backend, database and caching.
  • Anthropic: AI translation/explanation generation.
  • Apple: payments, optional Sign in with Apple.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status processing.

These providers may process data outside the EU under appropriate safeguards (e.g. EU Standard Contractual Clauses). We use them only to deliver the service described here.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

We process data to perform the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b)) and for our legitimate interest in a secure, working, abuse-free app (Art. 6(1)(f)). Where you opt into Sign in with Apple, that is your choice.

7. Your rights

You can request access, correction, export, or deletion of your data, and you can object to processing. The fastest route is in-app account deletion (see Account & Data Deletion) or by emailing us. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Children

Capendo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to backend data is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data surface intentionally small.

10. Changes

We will update this page and the “Last updated” date when our practices change. Material changes will be surfaced in the app where appropriate.

Questions or requests: dpw.vanderhoeven.tech@gmail.com.
